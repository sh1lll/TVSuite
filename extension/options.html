TVSuite options

All TVSuite settings (skins, sound, risk, capture…) are on their own page. This page holds accounts, sites and data.

AI provider for generated skins

Your key is stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local) and is used only by the extension's background worker when you press Generate. Only your text prompt is sent, never chart data.

My media skins

Use your own video loops, GIFs or images as skins (MP4, WebM, MOV, GIF, PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF; up to 300 MB). Files are stored in this browser only and never uploaded.

Discord & Telegram

Send screenshot cards, journal entries, risk alerts and trade recordings to your own Discord channel or Telegram chat. The webhook and bot token are stored only in this browser and used only by TVSuite's background worker. Choose what gets sent automatically in the panel: Capture → Share.

Discord: channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New webhook → Copy URL. Files up to 10 MB.

Telegram: create a bot with @BotFather, send it any message, then press “Find my chat id”. Files up to 50 MB.

Room lighting

Your lights follow the trading session, pulse on fills and alerts, and turn red when a Risk Guard limit is hit. Turn it on and choose colours in the panel: Sound → Lights. TVSuite talks to your devices from its background worker; nothing goes through a TVSuite server.

Desktop notifications

Mirror TVSuite sound events (trade entered/closed, alerts, price levels, sessions) as system notifications while the chart tab is in the background. Clicking one brings the chart back.

Other sites

Run TVSuite on platforms that embed TradingView charts (e.g. TopstepX/ProjectX, broker web platforms) or on any canvas dashboard. Chrome asks you to grant access to each site. Some prop firms restrict browser extensions on evaluation/funded accounts, check your firm's rules.

Data

Exports settings, your skin library and template snapshots (not your API key).
